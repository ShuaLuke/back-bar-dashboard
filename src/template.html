Overview

Click a cell to list the drinks that use both ingredients.

  • Unofficial. This is an independent fan project. It isn't affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to José Andrés, José Andrés Group or any of the restaurants. Restaurant names belong to their owners.
  • Menus and prices change often. Treat everything here as a snapshot and check with the restaurant before you go.
  • The “With cost data” tab uses made-up, adjustable cost assumptions to show what real cost data would make possible. Those numbers are not the restaurants' costs or margins.
  • Source: each restaurant's own online beverage menu, read on Oct 13, 2026: Oyamel, Jaleo, Zaytinya, China Chilcano and Bazaar Meat (listed on The Bazaar's DC page).
  • Prices are by the glass. Pitchers, flights, straight pours, wine and beer are left out.
  • barmini isn't included because its cocktail menu isn't published online.
  • Brands are grouped into plain ingredients, so Hornitos and Milagro both count as blanco tequila. “Salt air” counts as salt. Garnish techniques (liquid nitrogen, aromatic clouds, carbonation) aren't counted. The Freezer Martini is counted as gin, though it's offered with vodka too.
  • Operations views use the menus only. Sales and cost data aren't included.
  • A frequent pairing means the bars use it often. It doesn't rank how good a drink tastes.
