Overview

  • Unofficial. This is an independent fan project. It isn't affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to José Andrés, José Andrés Group or any of the restaurants. Restaurant names belong to their owners.
  • Menus and prices change often. Treat everything here as a snapshot and check with the restaurant before you go.
  • The “Cost & sales” tool opens on a synthetic sample dataset: made-up bottle costs inside published bar ranges, the real menus and prices of three José Andrés restaurants (Oyamel, Jaleo, Zaytinya), invented specs, and seeded random sales for three months. None of it is the restaurants' real cost, sales or margin data. Loading your own files replaces the sample; the files are parsed and computed in the browser only, with no upload and no storage unless you opt in. Method, data dictionary and security notes are in the docs folder of the repository.
  • Source: each restaurant's own online beverage menu, read on Oct 13–14, 2026. No menu aggregators. The groups and restaurants covered:
    • Prices are by the glass. Pitchers, flights, straight pours, bottle service, wine and beer by the glass are left out. Sangrías and beer cocktails are kept. A few drinks list no price online; they're shown with “—” and left out of price math.
    • barmini (José Andrés) and Bluejacket (NRG) aren't included because their cocktail menus aren't published online; Birch & Barley closed in 2024. Groups are compared as their DC locations only.
    • Brands are grouped into plain ingredients, so Hornitos, Milagro and Cazadores all count as blanco tequila, and Cava and Prosecco both count as sparkling wine. “Salt air” counts as salt. Garnish techniques (liquid nitrogen, aromatic clouds, carbonation) and menu taglines aren't counted. Menu typos are kept as written in the drink lists.
    • Operations views use the menus only. Sales and cost data aren't included. Menu gaps and the “pairings other groups use” list compare against whatever restaurants are selected.
    • A frequent pairing means the bars use it often. It doesn't rank how good a drink tastes.
